# Jonathan James

**Creative technologist building at the intersection of AI, products, storytelling, and developer culture.**

I come from copywriting, advertising, production, and strategy. I use software to turn research, creative problems, and strange ideas into working systems.

I am especially interested in problems where software needs better storytelling and storytelling needs better software.

## Selected work

### [Gigsaw](https://github.com/Damnjjwtf/gigsaw)
A ship-first career operations system that turns job opportunities into research, proof builds, and evidence-backed applications.

### [GenLens](https://github.com/Damnjjwtf/GenLens)
Daily intelligence for creative technologists working in AI-accelerated visual production. It collects, classifies, scores, and synthesizes signals across tools, workflows, research, hiring, cost, and culture.

### [Big World](https://github.com/Damnjjwtf/bigworld)
A local-first decision system for making beliefs explicit, testing competing hypotheses, preserving evidence provenance, and changing action when evidence changes the belief.

### [A Halloween Story: Structure Lab](https://github.com/Damnjjwtf/A-Halloween-Story)
A two-person structural invention system for feature-film development. It combines independent creative input, a library of story systems, AI synthesis, mutation, voting, and exportable structure candidates.

### [Critical Speculative Fiction](https://github.com/Damnjjwtf/critical-speculative-fiction)
A Claude skill for turning a provocative “what if?” into a believable near-future scenario, a fictional artifact, and questions worth arguing about.
